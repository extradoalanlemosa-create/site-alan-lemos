Problem

Support meant jumping between systems

To reply to a customer and open a ticket, the team switched between the chat tool, the ticket queue and spreadsheets, and lost the conversation's context along the way.

What I did

The conversation inside the team's system

I defined the access and sending rules and built the inbox: filters by inbox, status and assignee, the full conversation, replying to a specific message, WhatsApp templates, internal notes, AI writing tools and opening a ticket from the conversation.

Result

Context in one place

The conversation, the tickets linked to it and its history stay together. The AI suggests and summarizes, but nothing reaches the customer without a person reviewing and sending it.

What the inbox solves

Filtered list

By inbox, status and assignee, with search and a counter of who is waiting for a reply.

Full conversation

Attachments, audio, quotes and delivery and read receipts like WhatsApp.

Reply, template or note

Free reply, approved template with fillable fields or a note only the team sees.

24-hour window

When WhatsApp closes the window, the screen hides free replies and offers a template or a note.

AI that helps write

Improve, fix, shorten, change tone, suggest a reply and summarize the conversation.

Ticket from the conversation

The AI reads the conversation, suggests the ticket and the tag, and the conversation stays linked to it.

Rules that protect customers and the team

  • Each person with their own account

    Each agent connects their own chat tool account, and replies go out in their name. The access key never returns to the browser.

  • Each person sees only their inboxes

    Inbox access is per person: the finance team does not see support conversations, and vice versa.

  • The AI sends nothing

    The AI tools only return text for a person to review. Sending is always a human action.
