Smart AI App for Zendesk

A native app in the ticket sidebar that uses AI to classify tickets, draft replies and build escalation scripts, reducing the time the team spends filling in and interpreting each ticket by hand.

Try the demo

From manual forms to automatic classification

Problem

Time lost on manual classification

The team spent significant time filling in forms and classifying each ticket (priority, reason, product) according to the SLA policy. A repetitive, manual process prone to inconsistency between team members.

Solution

An app with AI built into the ticket

A native Zendesk app, integrated with the OpenAI API, that reads the ticket description, classifies it automatically, suggests a title, generates a technical diagnosis, drafts replies to the customer and builds the escalation script for the tier-3 team.

Result

Faster, standardized replies

Shorter response times and more consistent classification and communication across the team, with the whole analysis applied to the ticket in one click.
