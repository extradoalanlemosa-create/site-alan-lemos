For study groups and solo learners

Medidor de Estudos

A performance-tracking platform set up for your exam, whether it's a national exam, a university entrance exam or a civil service exam: lesson track, timer, question bank and the group dashboard in one place. It works for groups that study together and for people who prefer to study alone, with the same level of control.

  • Lesson track, subjects and exam date configured for your exam
  • Timer with a floating mini window and time per subject
  • Group question bank, practice and mock exams
  • Group dashboard and ranking, with the option to hide your own numbers
Try the demo ↗ I want it for my group

The demo opens with a fictional ENEM (Brazil's national exam) study group, in Portuguese. Mock exams, invitations and a few other features are only unlocked in the paid version.
