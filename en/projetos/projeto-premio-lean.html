Lean Efficiency in Construction Award

A monthly ranking system for a construction management software company that recognizes client construction sites for excellence in Lean management, with automatic indicator calculation, results auditing before publishing, and a logged-in area for each site to follow its progress.

Project built for a Lean management software company for civil construction
See the logged-in area prototype ↗ See the award's official website
How the project started

From scattered spreadsheets to an audit trail

Problem

Monthly ranking with no audit trail

Calculating the monthly highlight from each site's Lean indicators (IDC, IDP, PPC, IRR, PPCQ) and deciding who makes the podium depended on manual checks: no history of who changed what, no safeguard before publishing a wrong number, and post-result tasks (logo, certificate, contacting the winning site) were scattered across teams.

→
Solution

Automatic calculation, auditing and governance

A Supabase backend calculates the month's ranking from the indicators entered for each site. Before any result goes live, the admin audits it on a dedicated screen: compares calculation runs, can override an indicator with a recorded reason or make a site ineligible, and only releases the month with a click on "mark as audited". Each highlighted site gets an automatic checklist of tasks per team, plus its own logged-in area to follow its progress, download a certificate and send a testimonial.

→
Result

2nd place in the company's internal AI competition

The dashboard is in production, it's the public face of the company's monthly award, and it replaced scattered spreadsheets with a single audit and data governance trail, the theme that took this project to 2nd place in the company's internal AI competition.

Under the hood

Technologies used

Supabase
Postgres + Edge Functions: ranking calculation, role-based authentication (site/admin) and audit trail.
Vanilla JavaScript
The entire logged-in area runs on a single HTML file, with no framework, calling the functions directly.
pptxgenjs
Builds the monthly highlights presentation as a .pptx right in the browser, when the admin clicks.
RD Station
Captures leads from the "Schedule a demo" form on the award's public page.
Claude
Support in development, in modeling the audit trail and in prototyping this page.
